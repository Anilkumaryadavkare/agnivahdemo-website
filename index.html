What we're building

Trade intelligence, connected end to end.

TIKS—Trade Intelligence Knowledge System—is Agnivah's AI-native platform in development. It is designed to help teams discover relevant information, organize it, assess its reliability, and monitor what changes over time.

01

Discover

Organize a fragmented landscape of trade, market, regulatory, and business sources into a more navigable intelligence workspace.

02

Assess with context

Structure findings and make source context, provenance, confidence, and evidence relationships visible for review.

03

Monitor change

Help teams track updates and surface potentially meaningful signals that may inform business decisions.

Designed for trust

Useful intelligence should be explainable.

AI-generated output is only useful when teams can understand where it came from and what still needs review. TIKS is being designed around evidence-aware workflows and clear boundaries between machine-generated analysis and verified conclusions.

  • Source provenance and traceable evidence relationships
  • Explicit confidence and disclosure states
  • Structured research and multi-agent workflows
  • Evaluation, human oversight, and review checkpoints

How we're progressing

A deliberate path from prototype to production.

We are developing the platform in stages, validating the product experience and system boundaries before expanding production data workflows.

1. Build the foundations

Develop core workflows, data structures, provenance handling, and synthetic-data demonstrations.

2. Validate the workflows

Test usefulness, reliability, evaluation methods, and human review across key product journeys.

3. Prepare for production

Progress toward real-world data workflows with appropriate infrastructure, permissions, and legal review.
